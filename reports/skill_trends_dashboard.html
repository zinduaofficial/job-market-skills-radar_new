The Core Finding

Kenya's risers and the global risers are almost entirely different skills. Four of the ten fastest-growing skills globally are AI — Claude, AI Agents and LLM, all up 2.8–5.1 percentage points — and none of them crack Kenya's top ten movers. What's rising in Kenya is enterprise and data tooling instead: Stakeholder Management (+7.8pp), SQL (+7.1pp), AWS (+3.2pp). And Excel, still Kenya's single largest tracked skill at 46% of postings, is now its fastest faller (−8.0pp) — the existing core is already cresting locally while the AI frontier hasn't landed at all.
